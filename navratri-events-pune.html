What Makes Navratri in Pune Special?

Pune comes alive during the 9 nights of Navratri with colorful Chaniya Cholis, vibrant Kediyu attires, rhythmic Dhol beats, and energetic traditional Garba circles. **Dandiya Connect 2026** blends traditional cultural roots with modern festival production to deliver an unforgettable night.

Frequently Asked Questions — Navratri Events Pune

What are the event timings for Dandiya Connect Pune 2026?
Gates open at 6:00 PM every evening. The traditional Aarti commences at 6:30 PM followed by non-stop Garba and DJ sets until official venue timings.
Are wooden Dandiya sticks provided at the venue?
Yes! Complimentary wooden Dandiya sticks are included with every online ticket booking upon entry validation at the gate.
Is there a traditional dress code for the event?
Traditional Gujarati & Indian festive attire (Chaniya Choli, Kurta-Pyjama, Kediyu, Ethnic Saree) is highly encouraged to enjoy the full festive vibe and qualify for daily best-dressed prizes!
Is the venue safe for women and families?
Absolutely. We maintain a zero-alcohol policy, 24/7 CCTV surveillance, female security bouncers, and dedicated family entry gates.
